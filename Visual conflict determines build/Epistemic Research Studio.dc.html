HOW THIS WORKS

The Coach will not write your question.

It reads where your reasoning currently stands and offers the smallest push it can — a reflection, then a question, then a structure, then a sentence frame. The move itself stays yours.

Work down the spine: what you noticed, what you wonder, what is in tension, what is unknown, and only then the question.
